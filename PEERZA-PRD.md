# 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

## 1.1 Introduction

PEERZA is a digital social and academic networking platform designed to connect university and tertiary institution students across Nigeria.

The platform provides students with a central digital environment where they can interact with fellow students, build academic and professional connections, participate in campus communities, share information, communicate in real time, access academic resources and receive academic support.

PEERZA combines the social networking experience of platforms such as Facebook with the professional networking and community-building aspects of LinkedIn, while introducing features specifically designed around the structure and needs of Nigerian tertiary institutions.

Unlike conventional social media platforms, PEERZA is built around the student experience. Its structure recognizes universities, faculties, colleges, departments, academic levels and courses, allowing students to connect and collaborate based on their academic identities while remaining part of a larger nationwide student community.

The platform is intended to become a central digital space for student interaction, academic collaboration, knowledge sharing and networking across Nigerian tertiary institutions.

## 1.2 Product Concept

PEERZA brings together five major areas of student digital life:

1. **Social Networking:** Connecting students, sharing updates, interacting with posts and participating in campus conversations.
2. **Academic Collaboration:** Helping students discover, contribute and share academic resources.
3. **Communication:** Providing real-time messaging and organized communities for student interaction.
4. **Academic Support:** Providing access to AI-powered academic assistance and learning tools.
5. **Community Management:** Providing administrative and moderation tools to manage users, academic content and platform activities.

These areas operate within one interconnected platform rather than requiring students to depend on several disconnected applications.

## 1.3 Product Positioning

PEERZA is positioned as a student-first social and academic network for Nigerian tertiary institutions.

It is not intended to be another generic social media platform or simply an online repository of past questions. It brings social interaction, academic resources, student communities and communication together in one environment.

**Product Tagline:**

"The ultimate social and academic network for Nigerian university students."

# 2. BACKGROUND AND PROBLEM STATEMENT

## 2.1 Background

University students in Nigeria rely on different digital platforms to communicate, exchange academic information, access learning materials and maintain relationships with classmates.

WhatsApp and Telegram groups are commonly used for departmental announcements, course discussions and sharing past questions. Social platforms such as Facebook, Instagram and X are used for general interaction and content discovery.

However, these experiences are fragmented. Students often have to move between several platforms to accomplish activities that are directly connected to their academic and social lives.

PEERZA was conceived to address this fragmentation by providing a unified digital environment specifically designed for tertiary students.

## 2.2 Problems Being Addressed

### A. Disconnected Student Communities

Students frequently lose access to organized communication with classmates outside physical lecture environments.

Departmental and course-based discussions are often scattered across multiple messaging groups, making it difficult to maintain organized academic communities.

### B. Fragmented Academic Resources

Past examination questions, lecture notes, course materials and other educational resources are commonly distributed through WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels and informal student networks.

These materials are often difficult to locate, organize and retrieve when needed.

### C. Limited Cross-University Networking

Students from different universities frequently operate within isolated institutional communities.

There are limited centralized opportunities for students across different institutions to interact, exchange ideas, share knowledge and build connections.

### D. Lack of Student-Centred Social Platforms

Most mainstream social networking platforms are designed for general audiences.

They do not inherently organize users around university identities, departments, academic levels and course-related communities.

### E. Academic Information Management

Students need a more structured way to discover academic materials based on their institutions, faculties, departments, levels and courses.

Searching through unrelated messages and multiple groups makes this process unnecessarily difficult.

### F. Limited Access to Integrated Academic Assistance

Students increasingly need digital tools that can help explain difficult topics, summarize learning materials, support examination preparation and assist with academic calculations.

These capabilities are often provided through separate applications rather than being integrated into the student's academic community.

## 2.3 Problem Statement

Nigerian tertiary students lack a unified digital platform that combines social networking, academic resource management, real-time communication and academic collaboration within an institution-aware environment.

PEERZA aims to bridge this gap by creating a connected student ecosystem where academic and social interactions can happen in one place.

# 3. PRODUCT VISION AND MISSION

## 3.1 Vision

To build the largest digital student network in Nigeria, becoming the default platform where university students connect socially, collaborate academically, build professional networks, learn together and grow beyond physical campus boundaries.

In the long term, PEERZA aims to connect students from tertiary institutions across Africa into a unified digital ecosystem that encourages innovation, academic advancement and career opportunities.

## 3.2 Mission

To eliminate academic and social boundaries for tertiary students by providing a community-structured digital platform that organizes academic resources, fosters student networking, facilitates real-time collaboration and improves student learning through technology and AI-driven academic support.

## 3.3 Product Philosophy

PEERZA is built around the following principles:

- **Student First:** Product decisions should prioritize the needs and experiences of students.
- **Connected Communities:** Students should be able to connect within their institutions and beyond them.
- **Accessible Knowledge:** Academic resources should be easier to discover, organize and share.
- **Simple Onboarding:** Students should be able to join the platform without unnecessary institutional verification barriers.
- **Collaboration:** The platform should encourage students to learn and grow together.
- **Trust and Accountability:** User-generated content and academic contributions should have appropriate moderation mechanisms.
- **Continuous Improvement:** The platform should evolve based on user feedback, usage patterns and changing student needs.

# 6. USER ROLES AND ACCESS LEVELS

PEERZA supports different user roles with varying permissions.

## 6.1 Student

The standard platform user.

Students can:

- Create and manage their accounts.
- Set up academic profiles.
- Connect with other students.
- Publish posts and interact with content.
- Create and share stories.
- Send direct messages.
- Join communities and groups.
- Access the academic library.
- Submit academic resources for review.
- Receive notifications.
- Use available academic support tools.

## 6.2 Moderator

A campus-specific moderator responsible for reviewing academic contributions and maintaining content quality within an assigned university.

Moderators can:

- Access their moderation dashboard.
- Review pending academic resources.
- Approve or reject submitted materials.
- Perform other moderation activities permitted by their assigned role.

Their administrative scope is restricted to their assigned university.

## 6.3 Admin

A platform-level administrator responsible for broader platform management.

Admins can:

- Manage user accounts.
- Review and moderate reported content.
- Manage academic contributions.
- Oversee platform activities.
- Access platform-level administrative tools.

## 6.4 Super Admin

The highest administrative role.

Super admins have full administrative access, including exclusive access to platform staff and role management.

They can:

- Manage administrators and moderators.
- Assign or change administrative roles.
- Oversee platform-wide operations.
- Access administrative features reserved for the highest permission level.

# 8. PRODUCT FEATURES AND MODULES

PEERZA is organized into five major product modules.

## 8.1 Module One: Identity, Authentication and Onboarding

### Purpose

To provide students with a simple way to create accounts, establish their academic identities and become part of the PEERZA network.

### Core Features

**A. User Registration and Authentication**

Users should be able to create accounts and securely access the platform.

The existing implementation includes:

- Email and password registration.
- Email and password login.
- Google authentication.
- Session management.

**B. Academic Onboarding**

During onboarding, students provide relevant academic information.

The existing onboarding process includes:

1. First and last name.
2. University category: Federal, State or Private.
3. University selection.
4. Department and academic level.
5. Username selection.
6. Connection-building process.

**C. Build Your Loop**

Build Your Loop is an onboarding feature designed to help new users establish connections before entering the main social experience.

The existing implementation requires users to establish five connections before the main feed is unlocked.

Its purpose is to encourage early network formation and improve the relevance of the student's initial experience.

**D. User Profiles**

Students should have profiles containing relevant personal and academic information.

Profiles provide a way for other users to discover and understand their academic identity.

**E. Session Management**

The platform includes multi-device session tracking to help users monitor and manage active sessions.

### Expected Outcome

A student should be able to register, establish an academic identity, connect with other students and access the platform.

---

## 8.2 Module Two: Social Feed, Interactions and Stories

### Purpose

To provide a central space where students can publish content, discover updates and interact with other members of the PEERZA community.

### A. Social Feed — The Loop

The Loop is the primary social feed experience.

It allows students to discover and interact with content shared by other users.

The product direction supports both campus-level relevance and nationwide student discovery.

### B. Posts

Students should be able to publish updates and share content with the community.

The existing implementation supports rich-text posts and image uploads.

### C. Post Interactions

Users should be able to:

- Like posts.
- Comment on posts.
- Reply to comments.
- Repost content.
- Bookmark posts.

### D. Stories

Students can share temporary content through stories.

The existing implementation supports:

- Image stories.
- Text or colour-based stories.
- Camera and gallery uploads.
- Automatic expiration after 24 hours.
- Story viewing.

### E. Connections

Students should be able to establish and manage connections with other members of the platform.

### Expected Outcome

Students should have an active social environment where they can share information, interact and maintain relationships with other students.

---

## 8.3 Module Three: Real-Time Messaging and Communities

### Purpose

To provide students with communication and collaboration tools that support individual conversations and organized group discussions.

### A. Direct Messaging

Students should be able to communicate privately with other users.

The existing implementation includes:

- One-to-one conversations.
- Real-time message updates.
- Unread message indicators.
- Typing indicators.
- Online presence functionality.

### B. Communities and Groups

Students should be able to participate in organized communities based on shared interests, academic activities or other common objectives.

### C. Discussion Channels

Groups can contain separate discussion channels.

The existing implementation supports channel structures intended for discussions such as:

- Announcements.
- General discussions.
- Academic resources.

### D. Communication Notifications

Users should receive relevant notifications about messages and other platform activities.

### Expected Outcome

Students should be able to maintain conversations, participate in group discussions and collaborate without depending entirely on external messaging applications.

---

## 8.4 Module Four: Academic Library and Document Management

### Purpose

To provide a centralized and organized repository of academic materials that students can discover and access.

The academic library is one of the major educational components of PEERZA.

### A. Academic Resource Categories

The library should organize resources according to institutional and academic structures.

The intended hierarchy is:

1. University category.
2. University.
3. Faculty or college.
4. Department.
5. Academic level.
6. Course code.

### B. Academic Materials

The library is intended to support materials such as:

- Past examination questions.
- Lecture notes.
- Course materials.
- Other relevant academic documents.

The exact supported resource categories and upload policies should be finalized as the library develops.

### C. Resource Discovery

Students should be able to navigate the academic hierarchy and find resources relevant to their courses and institutions.

### D. In-App PDF Viewer

Students should be able to read supported PDF documents directly within PEERZA.

The existing viewer includes document navigation and viewing controls such as zoom, pan, rotation and page thumbnails.

### E. Student Contributions

Students should be able to contribute academic resources.

The existing contribution process includes:

1. Opening the contribution section.
2. Providing academic resource information.
3. Uploading a PDF.
4. Submitting the resource for review.
5. Waiting for moderator approval.

### F. Resource Moderation

Submitted resources enter a pending state.

A moderator or authorized administrator reviews the material and approves or rejects it.

Approved resources become available in the public academic library.

### Expected Outcome

Students should have a structured and accessible place to discover, read and contribute academic materials.

---

## 8.5 Module Five: Administrative Hub and Platform Governance

### Purpose

To provide tools for managing users, academic contributions, platform activities and content moderation.

### A. Administrative Dashboard

Authorized administrators should have access to an overview of platform activities and relevant statistics.

### B. User Management

Administrators should be able to view and manage user accounts according to their permission levels.

### C. Role Management

The platform should support different administrative roles, including student, moderator, admin and super admin.

### D. Academic Resource Moderation

Authorized personnel should be able to review submitted academic materials and manage their approval status.

### E. Reports and Content Moderation

The administrative system should provide a way to review reported content and take appropriate moderation actions.

### F. Campus-Specific Moderation

Moderators should only manage academic contributions within their assigned university.

### Expected Outcome

PEERZA should maintain a manageable and accountable platform environment as its user base and academic content grow.

# 9. AI ACADEMIC ASSISTANT

## 9.1 Purpose

The AI Academic Assistant is intended to provide students with intelligent academic support directly within PEERZA.

Rather than requiring students to leave the platform to seek assistance from external AI applications, PEERZA aims to integrate academic support into its learning environment.

## 9.2 Intended Capabilities

The proposed assistant should support:

- Explaining difficult academic concepts.
- Summarizing learning materials.
- Helping students understand course topics.
- Supporting examination preparation.
- Answering academic questions.
- Assisting with relevant calculations, including Nigerian university grading and GP calculations.

## 9.3 Current Implementation Status

The investigation identified an existing AI assistant interface.

However, its backend API endpoint was not found in the inspected codebase. Therefore, the feature is not currently functional end to end.

## 9.4 Outstanding Requirements

The following decisions remain open:

- AI service provider and model.
- Backend architecture.
- Usage limits and rate limiting.
- Context and document support.
- Cost management.
- Academic response quality and safeguards.

The AI Academic Assistant remains an important product capability, but its implementation requirements must be finalized.

# 10. USER JOURNEYS

This section describes the main interactions users are expected to have with PEERZA.

## 10.1 New Student Registration

1. A student visits PEERZA.
2. The student creates an account using email or Google authentication.
3. The student enters their personal information.
4. The student selects their university category.
5. The student selects their university.
6. The student provides their department and academic level.
7. The student selects a username.
8. The student proceeds to Build Your Loop.
9. The student connects with suggested users.
10. After satisfying the connection requirement, the student accesses the main feed.

## 10.2 Social Networking

1. A student opens the main feed.
2. The student discovers posts from the community.
3. The student interacts with posts through likes, comments, reposts or bookmarks.
4. The student visits other profiles.
5. The student establishes connections.
6. The student publishes their own content.

## 10.3 Sharing Stories

1. A student opens the stories section.
2. The student selects or captures an image, or creates a text-based story.
3. The student publishes the story.
4. Other students view the story.
5. The story expires after 24 hours.

## 10.4 Direct Messaging

1. A student discovers another user.
2. The student initiates a conversation.
3. PEERZA opens or creates the conversation.
4. Both users exchange messages.
5. New messages update in real time.
6. Relevant unread indicators and notifications are displayed.

## 10.5 Discovering Academic Resources

1. A student opens the academic library.
2. The student selects a university category.
3. The student selects a university.
4. The student selects a faculty or college.
5. The student selects a department.
6. The student selects an academic level.
7. The student selects a course.
8. The student views available academic resources.
9. The student opens a document in the in-app PDF viewer.

## 10.6 Contributing Academic Resources

1. A student opens the contribution section.
2. The student provides the document's academic information.
3. The student uploads the document.
4. The resource is submitted for moderation.
5. An authorized moderator reviews the submission.
6. The moderator approves or rejects it.
7. Approved resources become available in the library.

## 10.7 Participating in Communities

1. A student discovers or opens a community.
2. The student joins or accesses the community according to its membership rules.
3. The student participates in group discussions.
4. The student accesses relevant channels.
5. The student communicates with other members.

# 15. PRODUCT RISKS AND KNOWN GAPS

The following issues were identified during the initial codebase investigation.

## 15.1 AI Assistant Backend

The AI assistant interface exists, but the expected `/api/ai` backend route was not found.

This prevents the feature from operating end to end.

## 15.2 Academic Library Data Completeness

The academic library is partially implemented and includes a hardcoded course fallback.

A more reliable academic course catalogue and data management process is required.

## 15.3 Password Recovery

The password recovery interface is currently a placeholder.

A complete account recovery workflow is required.

## 15.4 Academic Data Isolation

The investigation identified that existing database policies allow public cross-university reading in certain areas.

The intended privacy and access boundaries for university-specific information must be reviewed and properly enforced.

## 15.5 Push Notification Security

The investigation identified concerns around authorization of push notification endpoints.

These endpoints require a dedicated security review.

## 15.6 Administrative Access Control

Administrative access should be enforced securely on the server and database, not solely through client-side navigation or interface restrictions.

## 15.7 Product Scope Alignment

Some capabilities, including the academic library, events and Android packaging, have evolved beyond the original roadmap.

The official product roadmap should be updated to reflect the actual product direction.

Potential areas of future development include:

- More advanced academic collaboration.
- AI-powered learning and academic productivity tools.
- Expanded university and departmental communities.
- Student career and professional networking.
- Campus events and opportunities.
- Institutional partnerships.
- Expanded coverage across African tertiary institutions.

These possibilities represent the broader product vision and should not be interpreted as commitments to include every capability in the initial release.

# 20. CONCLUSION

PEERZA is being developed to create a unified digital environment for Nigerian tertiary students.

By combining social networking, academic resources, real-time communication, student communities and intelligent academic support, the platform aims to address the fragmentation that currently exists across students' digital academic and social experiences.

The product's central idea is straightforward:

Students should not need several disconnected platforms to connect with classmates, discover academic materials, participate in discussions and build relationships with other students.

PEERZA aims to bring these experiences together in one connected student ecosystem.

Its long-term ambition is to become a major digital network for tertiary students in Nigeria and, eventually, across Africa.

**PEERZA — The ultimate social and academic network for Nigerian university students.**
